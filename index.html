The Ultimate Free Digital Toolbox for Teachers

Welcome to TeachCrate, your new favorite destination for free digital tools for teachers. We understand that modern educators are busier than ever. Between lesson planning, grading, and managing a classroom, time is a precious commodity. That's why we've built a curated collection of simple, fast, and highly effective web tools designed specifically for your daily teaching workflow. No downloads, no complex setups—just click and teach.

Classroom Management Made Easy

Keeping students engaged and organized is key to a successful lesson. Our Random Student Picker ensures fair participation and keeps everyone on their toes. Need to create teams for a group project? The Group Generator instantly divides your class roster into balanced teams, saving you from the headache of manual sorting. You can even use our interactive Dice and Coin tools for fun brain breaks or classroom games.

Time Management & Pomodoro Focus

Time flies when you're teaching, but our Classroom Timer helps you stay on track. Whether it's a 5-minute warm-up or a 45-minute exam, our full-screen countdown is visible from anywhere in the room and beeps when time is up. For deep work sessions, try our Pomodoro timer to help students (and yourself) maintain focus and avoid burnout.

Assessment & Grading Calculators

Grading doesn't have to be a chore. Our Quick Grades calculator allows you to instantly compute weighted averages. Just type the grades and their weights, and the tool does the math for you in real-time. We also offer a simple Quiz Score calculator to quickly convert correct answers into percentages. And for reading assignments, our Text Analyzer gives you an instant word count and estimated reading time.
